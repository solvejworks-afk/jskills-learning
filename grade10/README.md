# JSkills Grade 10 — CBSE Board + NEET Foundation

Flow:
School Login → Grade 10 → English / Mathematics / Science / Social Science → topic → external activity → 20-question assessment → mastery → Board Support / NEET Foundation → Teacher Matrix.

Demo simulation topics:
- English: Analytical reading and evidence-based writing
- Mathematics: Quadratic equations and graph interpretation
- Science: Electricity — current, potential difference and resistance
- Social Science: Development, sectors and sustainable choices

CBSE Board Exam Support layer:
- Chapter-wise practice
- 20-question topic tests
- Case-based questions
- Assertion-reason practice
- Competency-based questions
- Timed mock tests
- Mistake analysis
- Revision tracker
- Board Readiness Index

NEET Foundation Level 6:
- Biology — cell biology, tissues, human physiology, genetics foundations
- Chemistry — reactions, equations, acids/bases, carbon foundations and quantitative habits
- Physics — motion, force, electricity, work-energy and numerical problem solving
- Scientific Reasoning — data, experiments, graphs, units and evidence

The NEET layer is foundation and diagnostic only, not formal NEET coaching or a predictor of NEET rank. The Grade 10 product should prioritise CBSE board mastery while identifying students for advanced science pathways.

External activities are topic-specific and provide a direct external link if iframe embedding is blocked.
