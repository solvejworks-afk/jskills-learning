# JSkills Grade 8 — CBSE + NEET Foundation Level 3

Flow:
School Login → Grade 8 → English / Mathematics / Science / Social Science → topic → external activity → 20-question assessment → mastery → teacher matrix.

Grade 8 demo topics:
- English: Tenses, clauses and effective expression
- Mathematics: Linear equations in one variable
- Science: Chemical reactions and conservation of mass
- Social Science: Resources, land and sustainable use

The 2026-27 NCERT Grade 8 book family includes Poorvi (English), Ganita Prakash Parts I/II (Mathematics), Curiosity (Science), and Exploring Society: India and Beyond (Social Science). NCERT's new Grade 8 mathematics edition includes linear equations among its major topics.

NEET Foundation Level 3:
- Biology
- Chemistry
- Physics
- Scientific Reasoning

The NEET pathway is deliberately a foundation/diagnostic layer, not formal NEET preparation or a prediction of NEET performance. It is intended to identify students who may benefit from deeper science enrichment and longitudinal support.

CBSE's 2026-27 skill curriculum also makes Kaushal Bodh mandatory for Classes VI-VIII.

External activities are topic-wise and include a direct link if iframe embedding is blocked.
