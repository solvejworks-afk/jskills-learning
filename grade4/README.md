# JSkills Grade 4 — Realistic Simulation + 20-Question Quiz

Flow:
School Login → Grade 4 → Subject → Topic → external educational activity → 20 topic-specific questions → mastery → teacher matrix.

Grade 4 demo topics:
- English — Nouns, adjectives & describing people/things
- Mathematics — Fractions, equivalent fractions & comparison
- The World Around Us — How plants grow and what they need
- The World Around Us — Animal habitats and survival features

Each topic has its own external URL and its own 20-question assessment bank.

External resources:
- British Council LearnEnglish Kids
- GeoGebra
- National Geographic Education

Third-party sites may block iframe embedding. The demo includes an Open in new tab fallback.

Before school deployment, validate each topic against the exact current CBSE/NCERT academic-year curriculum and the provider's current usage/embedding terms.
