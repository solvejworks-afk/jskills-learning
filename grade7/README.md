# JSkills Grade 7 — CBSE + NEET Foundation

Flow:
School Login → Grade 7 → English / Mathematics / Science / Social Science → topic → external activity → 20-question assessment → mastery → teacher matrix.

Grade 7 demo topics are based on the current NCERT Grade 7 textbook family for 2026-27:
- English — Poorvi
- Mathematics — Ganita Prakash
- Science — Curiosity
- Social Science — Exploring Society: India and Beyond

Demo topics:
- English: Evidence, inference and persuasive language
- Mathematics: Finding the unknown in simple equations
- Science: Acids, bases and indicators
- Social Science: Weather, climate and data

NEET Foundation Level 2:
- Biology
- Chemistry
- Physics
- Scientific Reasoning

The NEET pathway is explicitly a foundation/diagnostic layer, not formal NEET preparation or a predictor of future NEET performance.

The CBSE competency-based framework describes progressive Science, Mathematics and English competencies for Classes 6-10. CBSE also lists Kaushal Bodh as mandatory for Classes VI-VIII from 2026-27.

External activities are linked topic-wise. Providers may block iframe embedding, so every activity has a direct external link.
