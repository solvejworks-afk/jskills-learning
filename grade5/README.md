# JSkills Grade 5 — Realistic Simulation + 20-Question Quiz

Flow:
School Login → Grade 5 → Subject → Topic → external learning activity → 20 topic-specific questions → mastery → teacher matrix.

Grade 5 demo topics:
- English — Adjectives, adverbs & descriptive writing
- Mathematics — Fractions, decimals and equivalent values
- The World Around Us — Digestive system and healthy food
- The World Around Us — Water cycle and conservation

Each topic has its own external URL and 20-question assessment bank.

External resources:
- British Council LearnEnglish Kids
- GeoGebra
- KidsHealth
- USGS Water Science School

Some external sites may block iframe embedding. The prototype provides an Open in new tab fallback.

Before school deployment, validate each topic against the exact current CBSE/NCERT academic-year curriculum and each provider's current terms.
