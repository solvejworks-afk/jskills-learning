# JSkills Grade 9 — CBSE + NEET Foundation Level 4

Flow:
School Login → Grade 9 → English / Mathematics / Science / Social Science → topic → external activity → 20-question assessment → mastery → teacher matrix.

Grade 9 demo topics:
- English: Argument, evidence and analytical writing
- Mathematics: Polynomials and algebraic identities
- Science: Motion — distance, displacement and speed
- Social Science: People as a resource and human capital

NEET Foundation Level 4:
- Biology — cells, tissues, nutrition and human systems
- Chemistry — atoms, reactions, equations, acids/bases and quantitative habits
- Physics — motion, force, work, energy and numerical reasoning
- Scientific Reasoning — experimental design, graphs, evidence and variables

The Grade 9 pathway is intentionally positioned as science foundation and talent identification. It is not formal NEET coaching or a predictor of future NEET performance.

External activities are topic-specific and include a direct external link if iframe embedding is blocked.
